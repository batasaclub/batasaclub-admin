# Batasa Business Model

## Vision
Build India's Hospitality Growth OS.

## Positioning
Batasa is a Hospitality Growth OS for independent hotels, with membership as one capability—not the product.

## Core Value
- Increase direct bookings
- Reduce OTA dependency
- Improve guest retention
- Simplify hotel operations
- Deliver measurable ROI to hotel owners

## Target Customers
- Independent Hotels
- Boutique Hotels
- Hotel Groups
- Serviced Apartments
- Corporate Travel

## Revenue Streams
- Platform Fee
- Membership Network
- Corporate Solutions
- Premium Modules
- Marketplace
- AI Insights

## Product Principles
- Hotel First
- Owner First
- ROI Driven
- Operational Simplicity
- Security by Default
